There is a small piece of PCB information that we have apparently not requested from the ODMs before, specifically the Glass Transition Temperature (Tg) of PCBs they are using.

 

This was not in our earlier surveys, since we recommend that standard FR4 with nominal Tg around 130C is sufficient for 0.062” boards when our reference process is used (230-250C), and higher Tg material is generally assumed to be ‘better than’.

(For boards thicker than 0.062”, we do recommend higher Tg.)

 

Recently, there have been some questions about a possible difference in pad-to-laminate adhesion, between 130C Tg material and higher Tg material.
