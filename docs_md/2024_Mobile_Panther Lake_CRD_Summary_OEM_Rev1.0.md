## Slide 4

**Highlights**

- All customers perform TC/Shock/ Thermal Humidity test, but thermal test conditions were different
- Customers have some difficulty on testing Thunderbolt.
- Half customers have experience placing small parts underneath a taller part.
- Most customers prefer NCTF testable pins connected to ground plane.
- MSFT is using recycled solder paste. Dell and Lenovo are evaluating. Other customers don’t have any plan. Cost and reliability is their main concern.
- Half customers hope Intel don’t remove solder paste recommendation in MAS, otherwise they have to qualified by themselves.
- All customers don’t perform compatibility testing between solder flux residue and adhesive, only perform after-cure quality validation.
- Different customers have different understandings on the influence of adhesive.
