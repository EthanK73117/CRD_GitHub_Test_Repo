## Slide 2

**BGA Adhesive Usage**

- 2

- This information was capture during the Cannon Lake Platform Intro tour. These 10 target customers are focused on laptop & 2-in-1 manufacturing, so this Q&A is more toward to this segment.

- Update as of April 2017. Clarification from Lenovo: For BGA adhesives they don’t use a full underfill, instead they use “corner fill” (Loctite 3910 adhesive), which covers a min of 5 solderballs in all 4 corners. It could be applied at any of their key ODM sites: Wistron, Quanta & Compal.


## Slide 3

**Additional Info per Jagadeesh**

- 3

- I had looked at teardowns of Intel and non-Intel based tablets (back when we were still focused on Tablets). At that time, I noticed that all SoCs (Intel and non-Intel) had corner glue or underfill. So, I would safely say adhesives are common in laptop / 2-in-1 / tablets / cellphones, and adhesives are less prevalent in DT and servers. Cell phones tend to have full underfill.

- Additional Info per SA Moy

- For tablet, I think it is much more sensitive on mobility (drop test performance). Moreover, if our future is also focusing on LTS process, solder joint protection with using adhesive is a key. In this case, we also need to remind package platform guys that to consider keep out zone for adhesive (corner glue). FYI…. During LTS sharing, a customer even asking us what type of adhesive that Intel recommended.
