## Slide 3

- Background
- ODMs use automated equipment to load PCBs into pallets.  Clamping the edge of PCB is not possible.  This is driven by cost savings.
- Intel clamps the PCB to reduce BGA area warpage to <50um.
- PCB design is main influence on PCB warpage.  (Thickness, Stackup, etc)
- Status:
- BDW YTB clamp vs no clamp shadow moire showed 2X increase in PCB warpage for BGA area.
- ODMs are not enabled to measure their PCB warpage.
- Next Steps:
- Collect PCB warpage data on Haswell Compal & Wistron ODM boards
- CME to collect bare Compal BDW PCBs and Pallet design info.
- Modeling to determine impact of increased YTB warpage
- SMT build comparing clamp to no clamp

- Clamp vs. No Clamp


## Slide 4

- Background
- ODMs use the full range of 230 – 250 degC for reflow.  Multiple board designs are run on the same reflow oven settings to reduce changeover.
- Intel recommends 235-245 degC for reflow to provide the optimized reflow conditions.
- Status:
- Intel tested 235-245 for BDW MAS development.
- ODM data from Moy (CME) shows acceptable yields at both 230 and 250 for the BDW ULX BGA.
- Next Steps:
- Determine need for BDL DOE testing at 230 and 250 Peak Temp.
- Review ODM data from Moy
- Align on MAS communication.
- 235-245 degC or 230-250 degC

- Peak Reflow Temp


## Slide 5

- Background
- Yunnan Tin is a lower cost solder paste being considered by multiple ODMs for high volume assembly.
- BDL completed two SMT builds with Yunnan Tin with positive results.  The Paste Screening test for MAS has not been completed.
- Status:
- Only 1 or 2 ODMs use Yunnan Tin but CME projects it will gain MS.
- Internal alignment that Yunnan Tin will not be included in BDW MAS.  May intercept Sky Lake based on future ODM MS.
- Next Steps:
- Materail Property Analysis of Yunnan Tin - Lilia
- Track ODM usage by MS and ODM - CME

- Solder Paste


## Slide 6

- Background
- ODMs print faster due to SMT line beat rate.  Typically 80-120cm/min.
- BDL process development was completed with slower print speeds for achieving better print quality.  Typically 20cm/min.
- Need to align print speeds to ensure BDL stencils designs are usable in the HVM environment.
- Status:
- Cliff / Industry DOE was completed with a print speed to 80cm/min.  Print quality met paste volume requirements.
- Next Steps:
- Align future BDL process development to higher print speeds.  Likely 80cm/min
