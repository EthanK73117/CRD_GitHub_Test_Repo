Highlights
▪ Most of the customers follow Intel MAS to design land pattern and
stencil.
▪ 4 mil is most popular stencil thickness, half of the customers use
step-stencil on mother board WLAN SD1216 location.
▪ Most of the customers locate the module in corner or edge. No
preference on top or bottom
▪ Only Compal CQ and HuaQin scan the SN# and save to database,
and only Compal CQ’s scanner has ECC feature.
