# 2023 MAS Recommendations Adoption Survey Report

## Slide 1

**2023 MAS Recommendations Adoption Survey Report**

- Rev1.0, CQ&R CMEE Team, 2023Q4


## Slide 2

**Objectives**

- Data Collected From 4 ODM Customers/Sites
- Wistron, HuaQin, Inventec(ChongQing), Pegatron (ChongQing)

- Share Findings From CMEE Mobile Industry Data Collection (MAS Recommendations Adoption Survey)


## Slide 3

**Summary**

- All customers follow Intel MAS to design stencil.
- Half of the customers follow Intel MAS to select solder paste. ODM will also certify solder paste by themselves or follow OEM’s recommendation
- Most of the customers follow Intel MAS to set reflow profile. ODM will also refer to solder paste’s advice before refer to Intel recommendation.
- Most of the customers follow Intel MAS to rework.
- All customers don’t follow Intel MAS to select adhesives.
