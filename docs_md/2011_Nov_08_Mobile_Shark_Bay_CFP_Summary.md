## Slide 3

**Summary of Survey**

- 11/18/2011

- 3

- A). PCB Technology
- No standard size of board form factor, rectangle is common but the size is different. Quanta (Dell, Toshiba, NEC) is using dual boards.
- The Minimum Layers Count is 2 (from Compal) and Maximum Layers Count is 8 (from Inventec). 6 Layers are the most common and widely used by Quanta, Wistron and Inventec.
- The Minimum Board Thickness is 1mm (from Compal and Inventec) and Maximum Board Thickness is 1.2mm (from Quanta, Wistron and Inventec). 1.2mm Board Thickness is the most common used at ODMs.
- OSP is the only surface finish used by ODMs.
- B). Screen Pritning
- The Minimum Stencil Thickness is 0.08mm (3.15mil) and Maximum Stencil Thickness is 0.13mm (5.12mil). The most common stencil thickness is range from 0.12-0.13mm (4.72-5.12mil).
- Shenmao (PF606-P) is the most common solder paste at ODMs. Wistron-Lenovo is using Alpha OM338 which is suspected high risk for hanging call issue. Low Ag solder paste (PF629-P) is next trend coming soon (using for small OEMs now). [KOKI-Local OEM]
- C). Placement
- ODMs are using PnP machines which has 100% solder ball recognition capability.
- D). Reflow
- Different reflow machine type been used by ODMs. All ODMs are using N2 reflow. Inventec and Pegatron reply <1000 O2 ppm, others didn’t disclose detail.
- All ODMs are using reflow pallet. Aluminum is the material common used and only Pagatron is using Glass Fiber, Epoxy Resin.
- The Minimum belt speed is 100 cm/min (from Compal) and the Maximum is 160 cm/min (from Wistron). 120 cm/min belt speed is the most common used by ODMs.
- The minimum peak is 230 deg C and the maximum is 255 deg C. The minimum TAL is 40 sec and maximum is 90 sec. Majority is following soak time 60-120 sec and Inventec is following soak time 60-90sec.

- CME – Customer Manufacturing Enabling / Intel Confidential / Collected Under NDA                                     Mobile 2013 Platform Customer Feedback Package (CFP) Summary


## Slide 4

**Summary of Survey**

- 11/18/2011

- 4

- E). Rework
- Different Brand and Model of rework machine used at ODMs (Martin, DIC and Quick) . None of ODMs using SRT.
- 4/8 using solder paste during rework and 4/8 using flux. Same type of solder paste in SMT been used for rework (Shenmao PF606-P).
- 4/8 customers are using manual component placement and expected at least >85% yield. Pegatron’s reply manual placement and yield is 99.85%.
- F). Glue
- All customers have corner glue process and capability. Wistron is practicing dot shape corner glue and Inventec is practicing L-Shape. Others customers didn’t disclose details.
- Loctite 3705 UV cure is widely used. Other brand like Panasonic (ADE480D-1), DYMAX, Loctite 3619 and Loctite 3620 are also common adhesive material.
- Different brand of equipment/dispenser been used. Only Inventec reply it is manual dispenser. There is no details sharing by other ODMs.
