## Slide 7

**Observations**

- 7

- Nowadays, aluminum is the dominating material customers are using for reflow pallet fabrication.
- Other material usage should < 10%
- The pallet thickness is ~5 to 6mm, per CME observation
- Almost nobody use spring clamps because not able to adopt automation process
- Due to thinner PCBs, more and more customer introduce whole process pallet to support PCB (from paste print till reflow), to provide good support to PCB during print, SMT and reflow process
- The whole process pallet requests to use tooling pins and smaller clearance, to hold the PCB position precisely.
- When HVM, most customer use customized pallet to support instead of general pallet, due to the complexity of board shape, size, thickness and component layout
