## Slide 7

**Compal KS X-Ray Process Characterization Results**

- 7

- Harry worked with Compal KS to characterize their X-Ray process on KBL G42 with a DOE & Dosimeters. See the table below for the details.
- In Summary, All the legs passed the <12 Rad criteria
- Compal X-ray machine is Dage XD6500, a relative older machine compare to Intel's Dage XD 7500
- Compal's normal voltage setting is 100KV, and the machine maxim power setting is 2.1W
- Compal build a fixture with a 2 cm (20,000um) copper filter under HBM die to protect the HBM.
- There are total 5 DOE legs:
- Leg1: No filter/No fixture, focus on HBM die for 10min. For the FOV and Pixel resolution, Compal didn’t find the setting on their machine, so we just take picture to show how many balls we can see on the monitor
- Leg2: Use Compal fixture, focus on HBM die for 10min
- Leg3: Use x-ray filter, focus on HBM die for 10min
- Leg4: Use Compal fixture, simulate the normal product inspect (Zoom in/Zoom out, move around to inspect all the BGAs, tilt to inspect solder ball opens etc). Total used 7’03”.
- Leg5: Use x-ray filter, simulate the normal product inspect. Total used 6’48”.
- For leg 4 and leg5, although the time is shorter than leg 2 and Leg3, the number is higher than leg 2 and 3. Hypothesis the reason is that Compal is zooming in more often, to inspect the details up close.

## Slide 9

**Quanta CQ X-Ray Process Characterization Results**

- 9

- Harry worked with Quanta CQ to characterize their X-Ray process on KBL G42 with a DOE & Dosimeters. See the table below for the details.
- Quanta CQ have the same Dage machine in our CPTD. The Leg 1 and Leg 2 have similar data as we got in our CPTD line:
- Leg1: 3min without filter, Intel: 3rad, Quanta CQ: 2.71 rad.
- Leg2: 5min without filter, Intel: 9rad, Quanta CQ: 8.21 rad
- Leg3: Simulate production scan w/o filter, board zoom in/out and move around to check different component. Quanta used 16’46” finished 1 board scan and the x-ray dosage exceed the threshold.
- Leg4 and 5: By mistake, they put the x-ray filter on top of the memory die (BGA between filter and x-ray source) instead of bottom of the BGA, thus the number is very high.
