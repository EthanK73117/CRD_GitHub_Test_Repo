## Slide 2

**Executive summary**

- 2

- Total received 9 customers feedback: Inventec CQ, LCFC, Compal KS, Compal CQ, MSI, Pegatron SZ, Quanta CQ, Samsung, Wistron KS
- 4 customers use UV glue only (Compal KS, Compal CQ, Pega SZ, MSI)
- 2 customers use thermal glue only (Inventec, Wistron)
- 1 customer have UV glue, Thermal glue and underfill material (Quanta CQ)
- 1 customer use underfill (corner fill) material only (LCFC)
- 1 customer use under film only (Samsung)


## Slide 3

**Executive summary (Con’t)**

- 3

- Lenovo and it’s ODM (LCFC, Compal KS Lenovo Line, Wistron Lenovo Line) are only using underfill material. Most of project use corner fill
- Wistron (corner dot) and Samsung (under film) are applying adhesive before SMT, Lenovo (and it’s ODM) apply after FT (due to difficulty to rework underfill material). Rest customers are applying adhesive after SMT, before electrical test
- All customers use inline automated dispense. LCFC use manual dispense sometimes
- Nobody pre-heat PCB when applying glue
- All gules are rework_able except underfill material (difficult to rework)
- QC criteria are various customer to customer. A lot customers have silkscreen on PCB to identify glue area
- Only few customers provided their reliability requirement
- Side note:
- MSFT apply glue depends on rel test result, if passing rel test w/o glue, they will not apply adhesive (message from 2016 CNL intro visit)
- Apple applying UV glue at one CNL-Y project build in Foxconn CD (message from Jan 2018 Fxn CNL-Y MAS training)

- Corner dot

- Under film
