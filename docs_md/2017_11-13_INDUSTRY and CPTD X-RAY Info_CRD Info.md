**ODM & CPTD X-RAY: CRD Information**

- 2

- How do the OEMs use the X-ray? Do they use it to see package to board alignment and do they have a re-work process?
- CPTD: X-ray each validation board twice, once at Vitrox (automated tool) and once at Dage (manual, provides tilted images)
- ODMs: X-Ray process is very normal in SMT manufacturing (ODMs). Normally ODMs will do package to board alignment during NPI build first article, then random checks in HVM mfg to make sure no part mis-alignments or SJQ issues. Moreover, if detect failure at ICT or Functional Test station, ODMs will use X-Ray to confirm SJQ too. About rework, it is really depends on each ODMs’ SOP. Normally, ODMs will do X-Ray after rework process (to ensure good SJ quality) prior sending boards back to mfg line for ICT and Functional Test.
- Is this 100% sampling?
- CPTD: Yes, all validation boards go through 100% x-ray during development.
- ODMs: NPI build will do 100% for FAI (First Article Check). In HVM will x-ray randomly (not 100%). However, it is really also depends on OEMs customer request. Sampling plan is different per different product life circle, for example, 3 brds every hour, or 12 brds every shift etc… For server board, a lot of customers ask for 100% on-line AXI.
- Is the X-ray from the top of the mother board or bottom of the motherboard?
- CPTD: Generally validation boards have x-ray from the top, in Dage & Vitrox. Typically the shortest parts are on the top side (as seen by the machine), to use “high magnification”, which requires small clearance. There have been cases when full product boards (servers) were run face-down, when there were very tall parts (such as connectors) on the top side of the board which could interfere with the x-ray head.
- ODMs: X-Ray from the Top is most common.
- What is the equipment type used, dosage/power, time exposed of X-ray, Field of view (FOV) dimensions and location of the X-ray w.r.t HBM?
- CPTD: We don’t currently know this beyond the basic tool names, but we could research this more.
- ODMs: Please refer to the information on the next page.
- Do they use any filters?  (please clarify, are you asking if customers shield part of the board to not expose to x-ray?)
- CPTD: I don’t know, would have to ask, but from current knowledge = NO.
- ODMs: I don’t know, would have to ask, but from current observations = NO.
