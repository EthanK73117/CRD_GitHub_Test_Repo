## Page 60

Compal’s Learning About Why Black SM Not
Easy To Have Good MD Pad In Small Pitch
During PCB mfg process, the SM (Solder
Mask) will be easier washed away by
high pressure development-fluid, if the
solder mask dam < 3mil.
When it comes to black solder mask,
during light exposure process, the top
surface of the SM will absorb more light
energy thus cured quickly, lead to the
SM bottom side still soft at the end of
the process, weakening the capability of
SM dam to hold itself on top of the PCB
surface. The SM dam will be easily
washed way at the following process
‘high pressure development-fluid wash’.
So in our design guideline, we request at
least 4mil SM dam width for black SM
PCB. It limited the possibility to have MD
pad in small pitch BGA because MD pad
have much narrow SM dam compare to
SMD pad
