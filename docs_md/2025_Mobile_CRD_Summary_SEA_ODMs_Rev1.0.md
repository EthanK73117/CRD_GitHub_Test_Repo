**Highlights**

- We total surveyed 5 SEA customers – Compal VN, LCFC VN, Wistron VN, Quanta TH and Inventec TH, received response from 4 customers, Wistron did not response

- The volumes are ramping up, eventually could > 50% of ODM shipping volume

- At LCFC VN and Inventec TH almost all engineers are still Chinese, didn’t see local engineer
- Compal VN and Quanta TH are better on localization, saw ~50% of the meeting attendees are local engineer
