- Solder Paste Formulation ( Brand and Model)

- Shenmao PF606-P is still preferred by many ODMs.
- Compal is using Yunnan Tin across different plants.
- Wistron still prefers to use Senju M705-S101ZH-S4(W2).
- CTEs are using local brand solder pastes.
- MSFT (JDM1) mentioned that in progress of evaluating solder paste listed in MAS
- included Indium.


## Slide 5

- 6/1/2016

- 5

- CMEE – Customer Manufacturing Enabling & Engineering / Intel Confidential / Collected Under NDA                                     Mobile 2016 CRD Summary

- Stencil Thickness

- Customers have 3 mil thick stencil capability included some of CTE customers.
- Stencil thickness selection is based on package pitch and PCB pad size.
- This is related to stencil Area Ratio.
- MSFT (JDM1) prefers to use 4mil thick stencil with nano coating for SKL-Y.
- This is to add more paste volume.
- Compal and MSFT are using 5mil thick stencil for SKL-H package (0.65mm pitch).


## Slide 6

- 6/1/2016

- 6

- CMEE – Customer Manufacturing Enabling & Engineering / Intel Confidential / Collected Under NDA                                     Mobile 2016 CRD Summary

- Min. Stencil Aperture Opening  per stencil thickness

- With 3mil thick stencil, minimum aperture opening is 9mil.
- Inventec changed to 0.245mm (9.65mil) to solve HoP issue on SKL-Y.
- Customers feedback that 9mil stencil opening is challenging for paste release.
- NOTE:
- Possible stencil technology different between U.S.A vs PRC stencil vendors.
- Working with Quanta and Wistron on fine pitch stencil technology
- collaboration.
