# 2023 MAS Recommendations Adoption Survey Report Rev 2b

## Slide 1

**2023 MAS Recommendations Adoption Survey Report**

- Rev2.0, CQ&R CMEE Team, Q4 ‘23


## Slide 2

**Objectives**

- Data was collected from 4 ODM customers/sites
- Wistron, HuaQin, Inventec(ChongQing), Pegatron (ChongQing)
- The survey was sent to 6 ODMs, only 4 of them replied.
- Why were these 6 ODM’s chosen to participate?
- CMEE has an established relationship with the 6 ODMs.
- These 6 ODMs were part of the LNL-MX CRD earlier survey this year.

- Share Findings From CMEE Mobile Industry Data Collection (MAS Recommendations Adoption Survey)

- Given the sample methodology, these results cannot be considered representative of all ODM’s who receive MAS docs


## Slide 3

**Summary**

- All 4 customers sampled follow Intel’s MAS to design stencil.
- Half of the customers sampled follow the MAS to select solder paste. ODM will also certify solder paste by themselves or follow OEM’s recommendation
- Most of the customers sampled follow the MAS to set reflow profile. ODM will also refer to solder paste’s advice before refer to Intel recommendation.
- Most of the customers sampled follow the MAS rework recommendations.
- None of the 4 customers sampled follow the MAS recommended adhesives.
- ODM also need to take OEM’s recommendation into consideration when they select adhesives.

## Slide 9

**Next Steps**

- Impossible for CMEE to scale (by conducting census or representative sampling of ODM’s).
- Very difficult to know where Intel is shipping products.
- The list of who is building a product is not stable but fluctuates over time.
- Customers have multiple manufacturing lines.. which ones are we trying to ask about this?
- We do not have contacts/relationships with all the customers building with our products.
- Would be very resource intensive for CMEE (lack the resources to support).
- Attempting to collect “MAS recommendation adoption data”, on large scale, would be a logistical nightmare.
- We do not recommend we publish such an indicator on  a routine basis as an OKR indicator.
- People will falsely assume the data is representative of all our ODM’s… therefore it will easily become a misleading indicator.
- We will consider adding it to future CRD’s to at least have “spot” information on MAS recommendation adoption.
- Such (limited) info might come in handy during issue management / troubleshooting.
