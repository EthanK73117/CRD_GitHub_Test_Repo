## Slide 7

- 12/11/2017

- 7

- Adhesive on Intel BGA (Focused on Laptop & 2-in-1 Manufacturing)

- All major customers applying adhesive on Intel BGA except MSFT (adhesive decision is based on the reliability test results-to prevent SJ cracks)
- All major customers are applying adhesive after SMT but before FT test (to prevent SJ crack during FT test), except with the underfill process (it is hard to rework)
- Lenovo’s corner underfill covers a minimum of 5 solderballs in all 4 corners
- UV glue is more popular than thermal glue, due to less factory floor space & easy to cure
- *We know Samsung uses underfilm but CMEE team did not visit them
- Top DT customers (Gigabyte & MSI) seldom add adhesive on Intel PCH (one exception: Asustek)

**Adhesive on Intel BGA**

- 12/11/2017

- 8

- CMEE – Customer Manufacturing Enabling & Engineering / Intel Confidential / Collected Under NDA                                     Mobile 2017 CRD Summary

- I had looked at teardowns of Intel and non-Intel based tablets (back when we were still focused on Tablets). At that time, I noticed that all SoCs (Intel and non-Intel) had corner glue or underfill. So, I would safely say adhesives are common in laptop / 2-in-1 / tablets / cellphones (cell phones tend to have full underfill), and adhesives are less prevalent in DT and servers.

- Additional Adhesive Info per Jagadeesh

- Additional Adhesive Info per SA Moy

- For tablets, I think it is much more sensitive on mobility (drop test performance). Moreover, if our future is also focusing on LTS process, solder joint protection with using adhesive is key. In this case, we also need to remind package platform guys that to consider keep out zone for adhesive (corner glue). FYI…. During LTS sharing, a customer even asking us what type of adhesive that Intel recommended.
