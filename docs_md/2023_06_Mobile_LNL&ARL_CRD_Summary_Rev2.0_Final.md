## Slide 4

**Highlights**

- Only LCFC and Compal KS have paste jetting machine experience. 
- Only LCFC and Compal KS have LTS HVM experience. LCFC use full board LTS and Compal KS only use LTS on Intel BGA. SPI machine tolerance varies with ODMs. 
- Large EMI shield may cause open after soldering. Clip is a good option. 
- 5/10 ODMs have experience with IR tool. Only LCFC and MSI have capability of weighted load force during reflow. 
- Competitor’s BGA has better yield performance, due to the square shape, thicker substrate, larger pitch. 
- Competitor’s BGA also has DSC design, and half ODMs have Liquid Metal experience. 
- All ODMs have X-RAY, Cross section, Dye&Pry. 3/10 ODMs have SEM/EDS. 
